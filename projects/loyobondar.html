Case study

LOYO & BONDAR

One of Bali's leading developers — 15 years on the market, 100,000+ m² of real estate, three completely different audiences. I built the communication logic for each and directed a graphic designer to keep up with a constant flow of high-stakes materials.

Period December 2025 — March 2026
My role Product, Art Direction
Areas
Communication Strategy Presentations Art Direction OOH Social Media

Why

LOYO & BONDAR operates at scale: 12 residential complexes, 5 factories, hundreds of millions in real estate across Bali. At every level of the business there's a different stakeholder — investors looking at ROI, partners evaluating reliability, buyers imagining a lifestyle. A single presentation for all three is a failed product. Each audience needs its own argument, its own visual language, its own reason to say yes.

What

I mapped the communication structure across all three audience segments and defined what each needed to see, hear, and feel. I built business presentations designed as sales tools — not slide decks, but conversion funnels in PowerPoint format. I also designed pitch materials for investors and partners, ad creatives, outdoor banners, social content, and email campaigns — all under continuous art direction over a graphic designer executing in real time.

How

Real estate development doesn't pause. A new property launches, a new investor arrives, an event comes up in three days — and every time, the materials need to be sharp and consistent without starting from scratch. I structured my direction so the designer could move fast without losing the system. Over 100 slides in the first week alone. The key wasn't speed — it was having a clear enough product logic that execution could scale without constant intervention.
