Case study

Scoville

A sauce brand enters the ice cream market — vanilla ice cream with spicy pomegranate topping. A product that is a provocation in itself, designed for the Samokat shelf.

My role Product, Packaging Design
Areas
Packaging Design Visual Concept Retail

Why

Scoville — a brand known for spicy sauces — decided to enter an unexpected segment: ice cream. Vanilla ice cream with spicy pomegranate topping — a product that is a provocation in itself. They came to us by recommendation, the task was clear: packaging for the Samokat shelf that would catch the eye and convey the character of the product.

What

Developed packaging design from scratch. The visual concept is on our side, while within the strict requirements of Samokat for retail product design. Combined two contradictions of the brand — cold and spicy, delicate and daring — into one image.

How

One week from first message to final file. Briefing, concept, design, approvals, edits — everything fit into a tight work cycle. Samokat's limitations did not become a barrier — they set a frame within which there was room for character.
