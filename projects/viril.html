Viril

Why

A client from Europe came with an idea — a product for men's potency — and a blank slate. No name, no visual language, no understanding of how to communicate in a niche where most brands either shout or hide. They needed a partner who would walk the entire journey from the first letter to the final screen.

What

The name Viril, brand platform: voice, tone of communication, values. Visual system: colors, fonts, logic. Full UX/UI design of the site in Figma — desktop and mobile versions in English and Spanish. 16 screens in each version, not counting modal windows, pop-ups and states.

How

The niche required a balance — serious enough to inspire trust, modern enough not to look like a pharmacy reference book. I worked from strategy to details: first I found the brand's voice, then built the visual language, and only then proceeded to the screens. The design turned out to be laconic — and in 2026 it looks as relevant as on the day of delivery.
