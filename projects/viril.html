Case study

Viril

A brand built from zero for the European market — in a sensitive niche where most competitors either shout or hide. I went from a blank page to a name, a voice, a visual system, and 64 UX/UI screens in five weeks. Solo.

Date October 2024
My role Product, Brand Strategy, UX/UI
Client Europe
Areas
Brand Strategy Naming UX/UI Figma

Why

The client came with a product idea — male potency supplements — and nothing else. No name, no visual language, no clarity on how to talk to their audience. The niche is crowded with brands that feel either clinical or sleazy, neither of which builds trust. The core product problem was positioning: how do you talk about this openly, confidently, and in a way that actually converts? That question had to be answered before anything was designed.

What

I defined the brand from the ground up. First: naming. "Viril" — short, European, direct without being crude. Then: brand platform — voice, tone, values, what the brand says and what it deliberately doesn't. Then: visual system — color, typography, logic. Finally: full UX/UI design in Figma — desktop and mobile, English and Spanish, 16 screens per version plus modals, popups, and states. Every screen followed the product logic I'd set at the strategy stage.

How

Sensitive niches have a clear failure mode: trying to be all things to all people and ending up trusted by no one. I made deliberate choices early — who this brand is for, what register it speaks in, what it refuses to sound like — and held to them throughout. Strategy first, visual language second, screens third. That sequence is why the design still looks current in 2026, and why there were no major pivots mid-project. Five weeks, one person, zero do-overs.
