Areas
Merch Design E-commerce Tilda

Why

Gabar is a YouTube blogger with a multimillion-dollar audience. I worked in his team for four years: content, production, SMM. At some point, the issue of merch arose — and the task fell on me. Not because there was no one else, but because I understood the buyer and his needs better than anyone else.

What

Gabarshop online store on Tilda — with connected mail and Telegram for order notifications. About 40 merch items and their variations: socks, T-shirts, hats, pants, hoodies, ponchos, caps, jumpsuits, stylized packaging, cards, booklets, certificates. 80% of the entire catalog is my work.

How

The site was launched in 3 days — parallel to filming, in a mode of almost no sleep. I knew the brand from the inside: the audience, the language, the visual preferences — this removed all uncertainty and allowed me to move straight to the result. The catalog was built not as a list of products, but as a reflection of the blogger's personality — and the audience appreciated it.
