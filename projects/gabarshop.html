Case study

GABARSHOP

A merch store for one of the biggest Russian-speaking YouTubers — built from zero to one of the most stacked creator catalogues in Russia. I knew the audience better than any brief could describe. That's why the task landed on me.

Period 2020–2024
My role Product, E-commerce
Areas
Product Strategy E-commerce Merch Design

Why

Gabar is a YouTuber with a multi-million audience. I spent four years inside his team — content, production, SMM. When the question of merch came up, it landed on me. Not because there was no one else, but because I understood the buyer better than anyone: what they wanted to feel, what they'd actually wear, what would make them proud to own it.

What

I designed and structured the full product catalogue — around 40 SKUs and their variations: socks, tees, beanies, pants, hoodies, ponchos, caps, jumpsuits, styled packaging, cards, booklets, gift certificates. 80% of the catalogue is my work. I also built the storefront on Tilda with order notifications connected via email and Telegram.

How

The store launched in 3 days — running parallel to active filming, on almost no sleep. What made it possible wasn't hustle, it was clarity. I knew the brand from the inside: the audience's language, their taste, what they'd find cringe and what they'd find cool. The catalogue wasn't built as a list of products — it was built as an extension of the creator's personality. The audience felt that. 100,000+ units sold is the proof.
