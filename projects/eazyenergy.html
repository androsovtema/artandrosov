Case study

Eazy Energy

Launching an energy drink brand in an oversaturated market — where the only real advantage is knowing your audience better than anyone else. I had four years inside the creators' teams before this project even started. That's what made the difference.

Period 2023–2025
My role Product, Art Direction
Areas
Product Strategy Brand Positioning Art Direction Merch Event Design

Why

Aleksey Stolyarov and Gabar are two YouTube creators with multi-million audiences who decided to launch their own energy drink. The market is flooded — Monster, Burn, dozens of local brands. Winning here isn't about having the best formula or the loudest packaging. It's about precision: knowing exactly who buys this, why they'd pick it over everything else on the shelf, and what the brand needs to communicate to make that click. I had spent four years inside their world. No brief could replicate that.

What

I defined the brand positioning and audience segmentation — which subscriber segments we're targeting, what they value, and how the product should speak to each flavor's distinct personality. I set the visual direction for all six can designs and directed a graphic designer who brought them to production. Beyond packaging: I led the creative direction for merch (backpacks, tees, socks, badges), a 7-city tour across Russia, and sponsorship of racing events — every touchpoint had to feel native to the audience, not like branded merchandise.

How

Every product decision went through one filter: would a Stolyarov or Gabar subscriber feel this was made for them specifically — or does it feel generic? That's not a design question, it's a product question. I used four years of direct audience observation as research no agency could buy. Each element — from the can print to the race track branding — was grounded in that understanding. The visual execution followed the strategy, not the other way around.
