Projects About

Eazy Energy

Why

Alexey Stolyarov and Gabar are bloggers with multimillion-dollar audiences who were launching their own energy drink in an oversaturated market. I had worked with them for four years before that and understood their audience from the inside. When the task came to create a brand, the choice of team was obvious.

What

Full brand launch: logo, brand book, 6 can designs for each flavor, Tilda website with the ability to independently update content, design of refrigerators and packaging, management of social networks. Merch — backpacks, T-shirts, panamas, socks, badges, stickers. Design of a tour across Russian cities and sponsorship of racing events: stands, banners, outdoor advertising on the track.

How

Years of work with the client gave us the main thing — an understanding of how this brand speaks and who listens to it. The design was built not around an abstract "youth energy drink", but around specific people and their relationship with the audience. Every element — from the can to the merch — had to look organic in the hands of the subscriber.
