Case study

EmTech Invest × Davos

An elite investment event at Davos during WEF 2026. I defined the product vision, audience segmentation, and visual language — then directed a graphic designer to execute across 66 participants, 6 weeks, zero room for error.

Date January 2026
My role Product, Art Direction
Client MaYu / EmTech Invest
Areas
Product Strategy Visual System Event Design Art Direction

Why

EmTech Invest is a closed annual gathering of tech leaders, investors, and C-suite executives held in Davos alongside the World Economic Forum. MaYu handled the logistics and brought me in for the visual product. With an audience from 50+ countries, any inconsistency in communication would read as a signal about the organizers' professionalism. The stakes were clear from day one.

What

I defined the event's visual concept and communication voice — how it should look, sound, and feel across three distinct audiences: attendees, sponsors, and press. I built the system framework and directed a graphic designer to execute within it: 66 individual participant profiles, press wall, banners, badges, website assets, and social content across the full event lifecycle — before, during, and after.

How

The real product challenge wasn't design — it was resilience. Participants swapped photos, edited their bios, dropped out due to illness — all while production was already underway. I structured the workflow so that every incoming change had a clear path through the system without stalling other deliverables. When you have 66 stakeholders with individual expectations, the only way to deliver consistently is to build a process that absorbs chaos, not fights it.
